Approach

At times, Life can be complicated, confusing and overwhelming. Whether you're navigating relationship challenges, addictive behaviours, bereavement, or simply feeling stuck, having a discussion with a trained professional can provide practical insights.

My approach combines evidence-based counselling with practical coaching stratagies. Rather than relying on one therapeutic model, we'll draw on psychology, philosophy and practical strategies to deepen understanding, build resilience and inner confidence.

Values

I don't believe people are problems to be solved. I believe most people make sense once their story makes sense. Effective counselling should be practical, honest, collaborative, evidence-informed, respectful and individualised — focused on meaningful change rather than endless reflection.

I'm interested in how people adapt and find meaning through challenges. While I bring a clinical practice and education, I seek to meet you where you are on an individual level.

Frequently Asked Questions
01What happens in the first session?

We'll spend time getting to know each other — what's brought you here, what you're hoping to get out of it, and how I work. No pressure to have it all figured out.

02Is counselling confidential?

Yes. What you share stays private, with the standard legal exceptions around risk of serious harm to yourself or others — I'll explain these clearly at the outset.

03What's the difference between counselling and coaching?

Counselling tends to focus on understanding and working through difficult experiences, thoughts and emotions. Coaching is more forward-focused — goal setting, motivation, practical steps. Most people benefit from a blend of both.

04How long are sessions?

Standard sessions run for 50 minutes, billed at the hourly rate. Longer or shorter sessions can be arranged where useful.

05Do you offer online appointments?

Yes — all sessions are online, so you can access support from anywhere in Australia.

06Do I need a referral?

No referral is needed to book. As counselling doesn't currently attract a Medicare rebate, a GP Mental Health Treatment Plan isn't required either.

07How much does it cost?

Sessions are AU$90 per hour — see the Fees section above.

08Can I claim rebates?

Not through Medicare at present — counsellors aren't yet included under the Better Access scheme, though the ACA is actively advocating for this to change. Some private health extras policies do cover counselling; it's worth checking your specific fund.

09What if I'm not sure counselling is right for me?

That's exactly what the free 15-minute consultation is for — a low-pressure conversation to see whether this feels like the right fit before committing to anything.
